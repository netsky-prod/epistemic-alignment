# Assumptions

- **ASM-001 (proposed):** Import should not durably persist health data until a retention policy is supplied and reviewed. Validate with the policy/data-governance owner.
- **ASM-002 (proposed):** Imported records require authorization/consent and auditability appropriate to their sensitivity. Validate with product and privacy/security owners.
- **ASM-003 (proposed):** Source format, validation rules, and retry behavior will be agreed before implementation planning. Validate with the product/data owner.

These are labelled assumptions, not approved requirements.
