# Glossary
# Glossary

- **Current importer** — Existing product behavior that accepts CSV and stores imported records for 30 days (`FACT-001`, `FACT-002`).
- **Health-data import** — Proposed capability to accept health-data records; format, validation, and retention are not fully agreed (`STMT-001`, `UQ-001`, `UQ-002`).
- **Retention** — How long health-data records may remain stored and what deletion behavior applies. Open, not inherited from the current 30-day behavior.
- **Record** — A unit of imported data. The exact health-data schema is not established in this dossier.
- **Confirmed** — A human statement or repository observation recorded with provenance; it does not mean approved for implementation.
- **Proposed** — A candidate behavior or architecture choice awaiting human confirmation.
