# UC-001: Import health data into the product

Scope: Existing product importer and record store
Level: user-goal
Primary actor: Authorized product user

Stakeholder interests:
- Product stakeholder: import health data into the existing product with a clear result.
- Policy/data-governance owner: retain and delete health data only under an agreed policy (`UQ-001`).
- Privacy/security stakeholders: protect sensitive data and preserve appropriate auditability (`UQ-003`, `INFER-001`).

Preconditions: The actor is authorized; an in-scope health-data file or feed is available; the format and retention policy have been confirmed. The last two conditions are proposed boundaries, not yet agreed (`UQ-001`, `UQ-002`).

Minimal guarantee: No health-data record is silently stored when validation, authorization, or retention policy is missing; the actor receives an actionable outcome.

Success guarantee: An approved health-data source is validated, imported as records, and stored with the policy-approved retention and deletion behavior.

Trigger: The authorized actor requests a health-data import.

## Main success scenario

1. Actor selects an approved health-data source for import.
2. System validates the source against the confirmed format and reports any invalid records without silently accepting them.
3. Actor confirms the import summary.
4. System imports valid records and reports the result.
5. System stores imported records under the confirmed retention and deletion policy.

## Extensions

- 2a. Source format or records are unsupported/invalid: system rejects affected input, preserves the minimal guarantee, and explains how to recover (`UQ-002`).
- 3a. Actor is not authorized or required consent is absent: system refuses import and records no new health-data records (`UQ-003`).
- 5a. Retention policy is missing or ambiguous: system does not proceed to durable storage and routes the question to the policy owner (`UQ-001`).
- 5b. Storage fails after validation: system reports failure and does not claim success; retry/reconciliation behavior remains to be decided.

Business rules: Current behavior is CSV accepted and records stored for 30 days (`FACT-001`, `FACT-002`). That rule is not assumed for health data; `UQ-001` must be resolved first.

Frequency: Unknown; validate with product stakeholder.

Open issues: `UQ-001`, `UQ-002`, `UQ-003`, `ASM-002`, `ASM-003`.
