# ADR-001: Require an explicit retention policy before health-data persistence

Status: proposed

Context: The existing importer accepts CSV and stores records for 30 days (`FACT-001`, `FACT-002`). The proposed health-data import has a confirmed product goal (`STMT-001`) but no confirmed health-data retention policy (`UQ-001`). Reusing 30 days would turn a current fact into an unapproved policy.

Decision: Proposed: place a retention-policy guard before durable health-data storage; block and explain the import when the policy is absent or ambiguous.

Consequences: Prevents silent policy invention and makes the open decision visible; may delay imports until the policy owner decides; requires a policy representation and deletion behavior after `UQ-001` is resolved.

Alternatives: Reuse the existing 30-day rule (rejected as unsupported for health data); allow storage with a temporary default (rejected pending policy-owner authorization); store only transiently (possible recovery path, but lifecycle and security still require decision).
