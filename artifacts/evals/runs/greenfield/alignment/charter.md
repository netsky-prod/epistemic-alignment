# Charter

## Initiative

Greenfield multi-stakeholder service alignment for a reviewable billing handoff.
This dossier is the canonical source for alignment evidence before implementation planning.

## Goal

Produce a reviewable billing handoff that makes billing rules, ownership, assumptions,
and open findings visible to stakeholders. A decision is not recorded in this case.

## Stakeholder statements

- `ST-001` (human answer to discovery question 1): the sponsor is the product owner.
- `ST-002` (human answer to discovery question 2): billing ownership is unresolved; no
  billing owner is named or authorized.
- `ST-003` (human answer to discovery question 3): the goal is a reviewable billing handoff.

## Scope and constraints

- The service is greenfield; no existing repository evidence is treated as a source of truth.
- Billing rules are uncertain and must remain visible as uncertainty.
- The review surface is derived and read-only; canonical evidence remains under `alignment/`.
- No explicit human approval message is supplied for the issued snapshot, so handoff remains unavailable.

## Material uncertainty

`Q-001` (billing owner), `Q-002` (authoritative billing rules), and `Q-003` (human decision
on the presented snapshot) remain open. The first is a blocking ownership gap for a billing handoff.
