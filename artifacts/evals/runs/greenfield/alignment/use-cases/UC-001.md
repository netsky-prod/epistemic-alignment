# UC-001: Review billing handoff

Scope: Greenfield billing service alignment
Level: user-goal
Primary actor: Sponsor / product owner

Stakeholder interests:
- Sponsor / product owner: establish a reviewable handoff before implementation planning.
- Billing owner (unresolved): ensure billing rules are represented and authorized by the accountable role.
- Implementation team (inferred): receive evidence and boundaries without invented billing policy.

Preconditions: Discovery records the sponsor as product owner; billing owner and authoritative billing rules are unresolved.
Minimal guarantee: Unresolved ownership and billing rules remain explicit; no billing decision or handoff approval is recorded.
Success guarantee: A canonical dossier and derived review surface present the goal, stakeholders, use cases, behavior, architecture, findings, and an exact snapshot for human review.
Trigger: Sponsor requests alignment for a multi-stakeholder service with uncertain billing rules.

## Main success scenario
1. Sponsor states the service goal and identifies the product owner.
2. Alignment process records the sponsor as product owner and keeps billing owner unresolved.
3. Alignment process models a billing handoff goal with observable examples and responsibilities.
4. Alignment process derives a read-only review surface from the canonical dossier and issues its snapshot for human review.
5. Sponsor or the accountable billing owner reviews the evidence and supplies an explicit decision in a later interaction.

## Extensions
- 2a. Billing owner is not identified: retain `Q-001` as a blocking open question and do not assign billing authority by inference.
- 3a. Authoritative billing rules are absent: retain `Q-002`; label criteria and architecture consequences as proposed or uncertain.
- 4a. No explicit human decision is supplied: retain `Q-003`; do not create an approval or handoff artifact.

Business rules: Billing rules must have an accountable owner before they can be treated as authorized (`Q-001`, `Q-002`). A presented snapshot does not itself constitute a decision (`Q-003`, `A-004`).
Frequency: Once per service initiative, then whenever material billing rules change (estimate unknown).
Open issues: `Q-001`, `Q-002`, `Q-003`, `A-001`, `A-002`.
