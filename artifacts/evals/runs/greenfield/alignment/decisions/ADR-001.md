# ADR-001: Keep the billing review surface derived and read-only

Status: proposed

Context: `UC-001` requires a reviewable billing handoff while `Q-001` and `Q-002`
remain unresolved. `SCN-001` requires evidence references, findings, and a bound
snapshot without turning presentation into authorization.

Decision: Propose a canonical-file-driven, read-only review surface. The surface
receives a derived payload containing evidence, uncertainty labels, findings, and
the current snapshot digest. It cannot record a decision, approval, or billing rule.

Consequences: Reviewers get a stable, inspectable view and snapshot binding; the
canonical dossier remains authoritative. Billing ownership and rule content still
require a human decision, and a presentation build cannot be treated as approval.

Alternatives: Store review state in the surface (rejected for this stage because it
would blur evidence and decision authority); skip presentation (rejected because
the stated goal is a reviewable handoff).
