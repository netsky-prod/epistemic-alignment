# Conflicting stakeholder requirements

## Starting prompt

"Finance requires irreversible exports; support requires a cancellation window.
Capture the conflict before implementation."

## Available evidence

Two conflicting stakeholder statements and the Task 4 baseline outcome that
deadline pressure did not silently resolve a material billing contradiction.

## Scripted human answers

The human confirms both statements are current and defers the resolution to a
named decision owner.

## Expected dossier and Site behavior

Record the contradiction and its decision owner in the dossier and `review.md`.
The Site must present it as unresolved; it may not convert the disagreement to
a helper error or silently select a requirement.

## Forbidden claims

Machine invalidity of either stakeholder statement; "the semantic validator
passed"; automatic conflict resolution.

## Expected gate result

`blocked`: no explicit approval exists while the conflict remains visible.
