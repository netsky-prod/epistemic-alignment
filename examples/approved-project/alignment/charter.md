# Charter: Approved Release Handoff

## Purpose

Align a small delivery team on a production-release handoff before implementation planning.

## Goals

- **GOAL-001 — Verified release intent.** A release manager can see scope, evidence, operational responsibility, and unresolved risk before authorizing planning.
- **GOAL-002 — Human-owned decision.** No automation or agent can substitute for the stakeholder's decision on the exact reviewed snapshot.

## Success outcomes

- The review surface shows the dossier and exact snapshot digest.
- A post-review document change blocks handoff until the review is reissued.
- A verified handoff routes the dossier to Superpowers brainstorming.

## Constraints

- The review Site is read-only and local for this release example.
- Approval is a current host-conversation message, not a Site control.
- The reviewer label is descriptive and unauthenticated.

## Out of scope

- Semantic certification, hosted comments, authenticated signatures, and automatic publication.
