# ADR-001 — Keep approval outside the review Site

- Status: accepted for review
- Deciders: release sponsor and release manager
- Related: GOAL-002, UC-001, BR-001

## Context

A convenient Site button could look authoritative while lacking host-conversation provenance, authenticated identity, and a binding to the current dossier bytes.

## Decision

Keep the Site read-only. Show the exact snapshot digest and findings there, but obtain the decision as an explicit current human message in the host conversation. Let the thin helper record and verify the digest equality.

## Consequences

- Approval remains visibly human-owned and cannot be inferred from Site state.
- The interaction is less convenient than an approval button.
- The reviewer label is descriptive rather than authenticated; FINDING-001 remains visible.
- A future regulated workflow needs an external identity/signature system rather than extending the thin gate semantically.
